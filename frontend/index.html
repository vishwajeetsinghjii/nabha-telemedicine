How Nabha Telemedicine works

Simple access for patients and healthcare teams, with role-based services behind the scenes.

1

Create an account or sign in

New users complete the appropriate registration process. Existing users securely sign in to their account.

2

Access your healthcare workspace

Your account type determines the services and information available to you.

3

Connect and manage care

Book appointments, manage patient information or participate in teleconsultations according to your role.

4

Continue care with limited connectivity

Supported offline workflows can continue locally and synchronize with the healthcare platform when connectivity returns.

One platform, three healthcare roles

Each user receives access based on their verified account and permissions.

👤

Patients

Access personal healthcare services and stay connected with your care team.

  • Manage your profile
  • Book appointments
  • Attend consultations
  • View prescriptions and history
🩺

ASHA Workers

Support patients in the community and coordinate care with healthcare teams.

  • Register and assist patients
  • Record supported health information
  • Coordinate appointments
  • Work within assigned areas
👨‍⚕️

Doctors

Provide remote consultations and manage care for authorized patients.

  • Review patient information
  • Conduct consultations
  • Create prescriptions
  • Manage follow-ups
